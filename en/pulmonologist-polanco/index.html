Many of Dr. Lara's English-speaking patients live or work in Polanco: executives relocated to Mexico City, diplomatic families, and long-term expats. Getting there from Polanco — the honest logistics

By car

Take Paseo de la Reforma west, join Anillo Periférico southbound and exit toward Insurgentes Sur. About 18–28 minutes in regular traffic; plan 25–40 at rush hour. Hospital Santa Coleta has indoor parking at Saturnino Herrán 59.

Uber / DiDi

Set the destination to "Hospital Santa Coleta, Saturnino Herrán 59". A ride from Polanco usually runs $130–$220 MXN outside peak hours — often easier than driving if you're near Masaryk.

Public transit

Metrobús Line 7 south along Reforma, then transfer to Line 1 on Insurgentes and get off at Olivo or San Antonio. Total 35–50 minutes. Doable, but rideshare is faster door to door.

Skip the trip

Follow-ups, medication adjustments and reviewing test results often work well by video. Teleconsultation is quoted via WhatsApp.

Office hours: Monday–Friday, 4–9 pm — evening slots after 7 pm usually mean lighter traffic on Periférico. Full directions and map on the location page.

Sleep apnea testing when you live in Polanco

Polanco sits outside the home-delivery radius for the sleep study (home service covers addresses within about 7 km of the office). That doesn't complicate things much: with the in-office package — consultation plus respiratory polygraphy for $4,500 MXN — you're evaluated at the office, take the small recorder home, sleep with it in your own bed, and return it the next day. Results are explained to you in English at the follow-up. Details on the sleep apnea test page.

Questions patients from Polanco ask first

Is the whole consultation really in English?
Yes. The interview, physical exam, explanation of results and your written treatment plan are all handled in English. Dr. Lara also reviews medical records, spirometry and sleep studies issued abroad, and can prepare a bilingual summary for your doctor back home if you need one.
How long does it take to get there from Polanco?
By car, take Paseo de la Reforma to Anillo Periférico southbound and exit toward Insurgentes Sur: about 18 to 28 minutes in regular traffic, 25 to 40 minutes at rush hour. An Uber or DiDi to Hospital Santa Coleta, Saturnino Herrán 59, usually costs 130 to 220 MXN from Polanco.
Is there parking at Hospital Santa Coleta?
Yes. The hospital has its own indoor parking with access at Saturnino Herrán 59, San José Insurgentes. It is charged by the hour.
Can you do a home sleep study at my apartment in Polanco?
Polanco is outside the home-service radius for the sleep study, so home delivery is not available there. Instead, the in-office option works well: consultation plus respiratory polygraphy as a package for 4,500 MXN — you pick up the device at the office, sleep with it in your own bed, and return it the next day.
Will my international insurance cover the visit?
Care works on a reimbursement basis: you pay at the time of service (consultation 1,500 MXN) and receive an itemized electronic invoice (CFDI) and a detailed prescription to submit to Bupa, Cigna, AXA, GNP and most international insurers.
