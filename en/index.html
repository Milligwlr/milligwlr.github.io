Services

Specialty Services and Studies

Comprehensive diagnosis, treatment and follow-up for your respiratory health.

Pulmonology Consultation Most requested

A complete evaluation of your respiratory health with a board-certified pulmonologist: symptoms, background and medical history for an accurate diagnosis and a clear plan.

Home visit consultation

If your condition makes traveling difficult, I bring pulmonology care to your home with the same quality of consultation.

Telemedicine

A consultation by video call from anywhere; ideal for follow-ups and second opinions.

Preoperative lung assessment

Before surgery it is essential to know how your lungs are doing: lower your risks and arrive prepared.

Spirometry with bronchodilator Key diagnostic test

The gold-standard test to tell asthma apart from COPD: we measure your lung function before and after a bronchodilator. Same-day results.

Simple spirometry

A quick, painless test that measures your lung capacity; the first step to detect asthma or COPD.

Respiratory polygraphy

An overnight study that detects sleep apnea from your home, with an accurate diagnosis.

Automatic CPAP titration

We adjust your CPAP to its optimal pressure to eliminate breathing pauses while you sleep.

Fractional exhaled nitric oxide

A breath test that measures inflammation in your airways; key in asthma.

Lung ultrasound

Real-time imaging of your lungs, without radiation: pleural fluid and consolidations on the spot.

Thoracentesis

Drainage of the fluid built up around your lungs: it brings relief and lets us analyze the cause.

Chest tube placement

Continuous, controlled drainage of significant effusions to give your lungs room to breathe again.

Pleural catheter

For recurrent effusions: drain safely from home and reduce hospital visits.

Diagnostic bronchoscopy

A direct look at your airways with a flexible camera: precise samples and an accurate diagnosis.

Respiratory therapy

Guided exercises and techniques to strengthen your lungs and recover your quality of life.

Smoking-cessation treatment

A personalized medical plan with tools that work; I support you until you quit for good.

Lung cancer screening

If you have risk factors, early screening and nodule follow-up can save your life.

Chronic disease follow-up

Asthma, COPD, fibrosis: we continuously adjust your treatment for fewer flare-ups and more good days.

    Frequently asked questions

    What patients ask most

    Short answers to the most common questions before your pulmonology appointment.

    What does a respiratory check-up involve?

    It is a complete evaluation of how your lungs are working. It is ideal if you smoke, have a frequent cough or simply want to make sure your respiratory health is fine. It allows problems to be detected before they become serious.

    What is a spirometry test with bronchodilator used for?

    It is a quick, painless test in which you blow into a device to measure your lung capacity. It is the key study to diagnose asthma, COPD or other hidden respiratory problems.

    What does the medical smoking-cessation treatment involve?

    I offer you a personalized medical plan with proven tools to quit smoking for good. You don't have to do it alone: I support you throughout the entire process until you succeed.

    What is medical follow-up for asthma and COPD like?

    We review your progress together, adjust your treatment if needed and work to reduce your respiratory flare-ups. The goal is to keep your condition from limiting your daily life. Learn more about asthma and COPD.

    What is the management of pleural effusions?

    When fluid builds up around your lungs, breathing becomes difficult. I diagnose the cause, explain what is happening and apply the right treatment so you can breathe normally again.

    Where can I find a pulmonologist near Perisur, Roma or the south of Mexico City?

    Dr. William Lara Vázquez sees patients at Hospital Santa Coleta, located in San José Insurgentes, a 10-15 minute drive from Perisur, Pedregal, San Ángel, Coyoacán, Del Valle, Roma and Condesa. You can book your appointment by WhatsApp or online.

    What diseases does an INER pulmonologist treat in Mexico City?

    An INER-trained pulmonologist treats COPD, asthma, sleep apnea, pneumonia, pulmonary fibrosis, pleural effusions, pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary embolism, bronchiectasis and post-COVID-19 respiratory sequelae.
