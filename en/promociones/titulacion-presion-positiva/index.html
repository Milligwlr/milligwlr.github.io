What people ask most before booking

How much does a CPAP titration cost?
At most sleep labs the regular price is around $4,500. Online we have a promotion at $3,900. Message me on WhatsApp mentioning the promotion to reserve it.
Do I already need an apnea diagnosis?
Yes. Titration is for patients who already have a confirmed apnea diagnosis (by home sleep test or polysomnography) and need to determine the exact pressure of their CPAP. If you don't have a diagnosis yet, you first need a sleep study.
What exactly is included?
It includes a clinical-grade APAP device loan for 5 nights, a new individual mask, seal fitting, complete overnight recording, interpretation by a pulmonologist and a report with optimal pressure, residual AHI, leaks and recommended mask. It does not include an additional medical consult: it is only the titration.
What if I already own a CPAP?
Perfect. After the titration I give you the exact pressure value you should program into your device. If your CPAP is very basic (fixed pressure) we can assess whether you need to upgrade it.
