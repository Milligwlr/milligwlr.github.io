Frequent questions

What people ask most before booking

What does the program consist of?
A comprehensive evaluation as a smoker, in which we measure how your lungs are doing today and build a personalized plan with craving and withdrawal management, plus follow-up to sustain the change.
Why isn't willpower enough?
Because smoking is a chronic disease with a dependence component, not a character flaw. With a medical approach (plan + support) your odds of succeeding multiply compared to trying alone.
What if I relapse?
Relapse is part of the process, not a failure. We review what happened, adjust the plan and keep going. No judgment here.
Do I have to quit cold turkey?
Not necessarily. Together we set a date and the method that works best for you: sometimes it's quitting all at once and sometimes a guided reduction. The plan adapts to you.
I already tried on my own and relapsed, is this different?
Yes. Here there is a medical plan, craving management and follow-up, not just good intentions. Most people who succeed had previous attempts: a relapse doesn't disqualify you.
Does it work if I've smoked for many years?
Yes. The benefit to your body starts within the first hours of quitting, no matter how long you've smoked. It's never too late to start.
What is the cost?
The evaluation has an online price of $1,300 (regular $1,800); just mention that you found us online. If your plan requires additional medical treatment, it is quoted separately and explained to you beforehand.
How long does the support last?
Quitting smoking is consolidated over time, so follow-up extends over several months with check-in visits to adjust the plan based on how you're doing.
Where and who do you treat?
At Hospital Santa Coleta, San José Insurgentes (Benito Juárez, Mexico City). Care is exclusively for adults (18 and older), by appointment.
