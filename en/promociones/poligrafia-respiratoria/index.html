Are you at risk of sleep apnea? Find out in 2 min

The STOP-BANG questionnaire is a globally validated clinical tool to estimate the risk of obstructive sleep apnea. Answer 8 questions and find out your risk level.

0 of 8 questions answered
S
Snoring

Do you snore loudly, louder than talking, or loud enough to be heard through a closed door?

T
Tiredness

Do you often feel tired, fatigued, or sleepy during the day, even after sleeping?

O
Observed

Has anyone noticed that you stop breathing, or that you gasp or choke, while you sleep?

P

Do you have high blood pressure, or are you being treated with medication to control it?

Is your body mass index (BMI) greater than 35?

Is your neck circumference greater than 40 cm? (Roughly, if you wear a 16" shirt collar or larger)

What people ask most before booking

How much does it cost and what's included?
$4,500 (regularly $6,000) includes: pulmonology consultation, device, polygraphy, and interpretation by an INER-trained pulmonologist. Most labs charge $3,000–$4,000 just for the device, with no doctor. Book on WhatsApp.
Is an at-home study really valid?
Yes. Respiratory polygraphy is a type III study validated by the American Academy of Sleep Medicine (AASM) for diagnosing obstructive sleep apnea in patients with a high clinical suspicion and without serious cardiopulmonary disease. For most people with snoring and typical symptoms, it is enough to confirm or rule out the diagnosis.
What if I can't sleep with the device?
The device is lightweight and most people get used to it within 30 minutes. If the night wasn't enough for a valid study, we repeat it at no extra cost.
When do I get the results?
The report is delivered in consultation within 48 hours of returning the device, with a clear explanation and a treatment plan.
