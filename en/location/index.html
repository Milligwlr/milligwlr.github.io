How to get to the office

Four ways to get to Hospital Santa Coleta, San José Insurgentes.

By car

Saturnino Herrán 59, near the intersection with Insurgentes Sur. The hospital has its own parking inside the building. From Periférico Sur or Viaducto, allow 10–20 minutes depending on traffic.

By Metro / Metrobús

Metrobús Line 1: Olivo or San Antonio station, a 5–7 minute walk. Mixcoac Metro station (Lines 7 and 12) is 15 minutes away by taxi or ride-hailing app.

Uber / DiDi

Request a ride straight to "Hospital Santa Coleta, Saturnino Herrán 59". Approximate fare from Roma or Coyoacán: $80–$150 MXN at regular hours.

Without leaving home

If you live far away or your condition warrants it, telemedicine is available for follow-up and first-time opinions (except cases that require a direct physical examination).

Patients from all over central and southern Mexico City

The office mainly receives patients from the neighborhoods of central and southern Mexico City, all within a 15–20 minute drive.
